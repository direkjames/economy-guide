---
title: Launch plan
desc: A step-by-step plan for setting up a new economy, or rebuilding one you already run.
---

Follow these steps in order. Each one links to the page with the detail. Skipping the early steps is the most common reason economies need a reset later.

:::steps
1. **Decide what the economy is for.** Is money the main goal (a trading server), or a tool for buying upgrades? Write one sentence. Every later choice must serve it.
2. **Choose the currency.** One currency is simpler. A second, premium currency adds complexity, so only add it if you have a clear reason. See [Currency](design/currency.md).
3. **Set the hourly rate.** Decide what a normal hour of play should earn at the start, in the middle and late in the game. See [Pricing items](design/pricing.md).
4. **Build the price list.** Price every sellable and buyable item from hours of effort. Fill in a spreadsheet, not the plugin, first.
5. **Run the loop test.** Check every recipe and shop pair for a chain that makes coins from nothing. See [Stopping exploits](fair/exploits.md).
6. **List your faucets and set their limits.** For each one, write down the maximum coins per player per hour. See [Money in](faucets/catalogue.md).
7. **List your sinks and make sure they are worth buying.** Plots, upgrades, cosmetics, ranks and fees. See [Money out](sinks/catalogue.md).
8. **Add taxes and fees.** Keep them simple and visible. See [Taxes and fees](sinks/taxes.md).
9. **Plan the first week for new players.** Starter kit, first goals and a short list of cheap things to buy. See [Fairness](fair/fairness.md).
10. **Turn on logging and a weekly number sheet.** You need a baseline from day one. See [Tracking the economy](inflation/tracking.md).
11. **Soft launch.** Test with staff and a few trusted players for a few days. Try to break it on purpose.
12. **Open the server, then review every week.** Use the [weekly checklist](toolkit/checklist.md).
:::

## What to prepare before day one

- A spreadsheet with items, buy prices, sell prices and hours to obtain.
- A written list of faucets and sinks, each with a limit.
- A plugin list that matches your needs. See [Plugin ideas](toolkit/plugins.md).
- A staff rule for money: who can use admin commands, and how they are logged.
- A plan for what happens if something breaks: pause shops, freeze transfers, roll back. See [Fixing inflation](inflation/fixing.md).

## If you already run a server

Do not rebuild everything at once. Run the [health check](toolkit/health-check.md) first. Fix the biggest problem, wait a week, then fix the next.

> [!NUGGET] Nugget says
> Soft launch matters more than it sounds. Players find exploits in hours. If your first ten testers cannot break it, you are in good shape.
