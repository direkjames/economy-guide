Print this page or pin it in your staff channel. When you are not sure what to do, check it against these rules.

## The rules

> [!RULE] 1. Every coin that comes in needs a way out
> Never add a faucet without deciding which sink will balance it.

> [!RULE] 2. Aim for sinks of 80% to 100% of faucets
> Measured over a week, on a settled server. A brand-new server can run at 60% to 80% while players are still building up.

> [!RULE] 3. Watch money per player, not total money
> Total money grows when more players join, and that is fine. What matters is the average per active player. Keep it within about 5% of last week once the server settles.

> [!RULE] 4. Price in hours, not coins
> Decide what an hour of play should earn. Price every item as hours to obtain times that rate.

> [!RULE] 5. Buy price must always be higher than sell price
> Admin shops should pay players 20% to 40% of what they charge. The gap is a built-in tax and it also blocks loops.

> [!RULE] 6. Test every loop before launch
> If any chain of buying, crafting and selling ends with more coins than it started with, players will find it within a day.

> [!RULE] 7. Add diminishing returns
> The 1,000th item sold in a day should pay less than the first. Daily caps, falling prices or stock limits all work.

> [!RULE] 8. Tax lightly and visibly
> 2% to 5% on player trades. Small enough that nobody avoids it, big enough to matter over thousands of trades.

> [!RULE] 9. Sinks should feel like spending, not losing
> Players accept paying for a plot, a cosmetic or an upgrade. They hate a hidden fee. Give each sink something to look at.

> [!RULE] 10. Rich players need things to buy
> If a rich player has nothing worth buying, their money sits and the economy stalls. Add prestige sinks: cosmetics, large builds, titles and limited items.

> [!RULE] 11. Help new players, never feed them money
> Starter kits, early quests and cheap first goals work better than big starting balances.

> [!RULE] 12. Measure every week, change one thing at a time
> Write down the numbers. Change one lever. Wait a week. Then judge.

> [!RULE] 13. Never rebalance in secret
> Announce changes, give a short notice period and explain the reason. Silent nerfs destroy trust.

> [!RULE] 14. Log everything money touches
> Admin commands, big transfers, shop sales and plugin errors. You cannot fix what you cannot see.

> [!RULE] 15. Keep staff out of the economy
> Staff should never give themselves or friends money. Use a separate account for testing and log every admin command.

## Numbers to remember

| Thing | Starting point |
| --- | --- |
| Sinks as a share of faucets (settled server) | 80% to 100% |
| Admin shop sell price vs buy price | 20% to 40% |
| Player trade tax | 2% to 5% |
| Auction listing or sale fee | 3% to 10% |
| Bank interest | Small, capped, and only on a limited balance |
| Starting balance | About 30 to 60 minutes of early-game earnings |
| Weekly change in money per player | Within 5% when stable, up to 15% when young |
| Average balance | 7 to 60 days of a normal player's income |
| Share of money held by the richest 10% | Under 50% is healthy, over 70% is a warning |

> [!WARNING] Starting points, not laws
> These are common, battle-tested ranges, not universal truths. Your server has its own player count, speed and style. Start here, measure and adjust.

## When something looks wrong

1. Open the [health check](toolkit/health-check.md) and find which number is off.
2. Read [spotting inflation](inflation/spotting.md) to find the cause.
3. Use the [fixing playbook](inflation/fixing.md) and pick the gentlest lever that works.
