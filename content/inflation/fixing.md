title: Fixing inflation
desc: A calm playbook, from gentle nudges to emergency measures, plus how to handle deflation and resets.
---

Use the **gentlest lever that works**. Players forgive a small nudge. They do not forgive a surprise.

## The ladder of responses

| Step | What you do | When to use it | Player impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Add or improve a sink players want | Mild inflation, caught early | None, feels like a new feature |
| 2 | Tighten a faucet slightly (caps, sell price) | One faucet clearly leads | Small |
| 3 | Raise taxes and fees by a point or two | You need steady drain | Small to medium |
| 4 | Add a temporary event sink (limited shop, auction) | You need a one-off drain | Fun |
| 5 | Remove or rework a broken faucet | Faucet is clearly broken | Medium |
| 6 | Wealth tax or upkeep above a threshold | Hoarding is the problem | Medium to high |
| 7 | Price reset: change shop and item prices | Prices drifted a lot | High |
| 8 | Currency reform: convert balances (for example divide by 10) | Numbers are absurd | High |
| 9 | Economy reset or season wipe | Trust in the money is gone | Very high |

Move up the ladder one step at a time and measure for a week after each change.

## Step by step: your first response

1. **Stop the bleeding.** If an exploit or a bug is adding money, pause the shop, the job or the faucet while you fix it.
2. **Find the cause.** Use [Spotting inflation](inflation/spotting.md).
3. **Fix the cause, not the symptom.** If a farm sells too much, cap the farm, not every sale.
4. **Add a sink or two** that players like. A sale on cosmetics or a limited-time shop works well.
5. **Announce what you changed.** Short, honest and without blame. See the template below.
6. **Measure again** in 7 days with the [health check](toolkit/health-check.md).

## Handling an exploit

1. Pause the exploited feature immediately.
2. Check logs for who used it and how much they earned.
3. Remove illegally gained money. Take it back as a balance adjustment, not a ban, unless there is clear abuse.
4. Decide a fair policy: first offense warning and rollback, repeated abuse is a ban.
5. Close the hole, then reopen the feature.
6. Tell players what happened in a short post. See [Stopping exploits](fair/exploits.md).

## Fixing deflation

Players broke and quitting is as bad as inflation.

- Raise rewards, or reduce the price of core items.
- Add small, friendly faucets: daily rewards, easy quests, a weekend bonus.
- Remove or soften the harshest fees.
- Check that early game still has quick wins.

## Price resets and currency reform

If prices have drifted too far, you can:

- **Reprice shops.** Players adapt in a week or two.
- **Reform the currency.** For example, divide every balance and every price by 10. Everyone keeps their relative wealth and the numbers become readable again.
- **Reset the economy.** A fresh start. Last resort.

> [!WARNING] Resets cost trust
> A reset can fix the numbers, but it cannot fix the habit that created the problem. Fix the **system** first or the same thing happens again.

## Sample announcement

> **Economy update.** Over the last two weeks, prices rose faster than we want. We found the cause: sell prices for farm items were too high. From Monday, farm items sell for less and there is a daily sell limit. To balance it, we are adding a limited-time cosmetics shop. We will review the numbers again in a week and share the result.

Short, clear and no blame.

## After the fix

- Record the numbers before and after
- Write what you changed, the date and the reason in a log
- Re-run the [health check](toolkit/health-check.md) next week
- Add a note about the root cause so future staff learn from it
