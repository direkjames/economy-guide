## How to get the numbers

You do not need to be exact. Rough numbers give a useful answer.

| Number | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Active players | Your plugin's player count, or logins in the last 7 days |
| Total money | The sum of all balances. Many economy plugins show it, or add up the top balances and estimate the rest |
| Money created per day | Payouts from jobs, shop sales, rewards, crates, interest and so on, averaged over the week |
| Money removed per day | Taxes, fees, rent, shop purchases, upgrades, cosmetics and so on, averaged over the week |
| Richest 10% share | Add up the top 10% of balances and divide by the total |

## What the verdict means

| Check | Healthy | Watch | Act |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sinks vs faucets | 80% to 120% | 60% to 80%, or 120% to 150% | Under 60%, or over 150% |
| Money per player, week over week | Within 5% | 5% to 15% | Over 15% either way |
| Average balance, in days of income | 7 to 60 days | Under 7, or 60 to 90 | Over 90 days |
| Richest 10% share | Under 50% | 50% to 70% | Over 70% |

> [!NUGGET] Nugget says
> Run this every week at the same time and keep the results in your sheet. One reading tells you where you are. A month of readings tells you where you are going.

Your numbers are saved in this browser only, so you can come back next week and update them.
