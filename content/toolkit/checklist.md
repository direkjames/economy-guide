title: Weekly checklist
desc: A routine for staff. Tick things off as you go. Your progress is saved in this browser.
tool: checklist
---

## Every day (5 minutes)

- Skim the money log for unusually large transfers or payouts
- Check the chat and reports for "I found a bug" or "that is too cheap"
- Look at the top 5 balance changes of the day
- Make sure no plugin errors mention money or economy

## Every week (30 minutes)

- Write down active players and total money in the weekly sheet
- Work out money per player and compare with last week
- Add up faucets and sinks and work out the sinks vs faucets percentage
- Run the health check and save the result
- Record the median price of your basket of common items
- Review the top 10 earners and the top 10 new balances for suspicious accounts
- Check that every event or giveaway stayed inside its budget
- List what you changed this week and why

## Every month (1 hour)

- Review all shop prices in the spreadsheet and fix anything out of line
- Re-run the loop test on any recipe or item you added
- Check the richest 10% share and the wealth gap
- Check that new players still reach their first goals within a session
- Review tax and fee totals and decide whether the rates still make sense
- Look at the most-used sinks and the least-used sinks
- Update the wiki or rules page with any new rates, caps or prices
- Post a short economy report for players

## Before any change

- Write down the number you want to move and by how much
- Change only one lever at a time
- Announce the change in advance
- Set a date to review the result

## After an incident

- Stop the faucet or feature involved
- Find who used it and how much was made
- Remove illegal gains fairly
- Fix the cause and test the fix
- Tell players what happened and what changed
- Add a note to the log so the next person can learn from it
