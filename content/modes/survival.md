---
title: Survival
desc: Economy advice for a normal survival world with big maps, farms, villagers and land.
---

Survival has a real world, real land and real machines. Money here has to compete with a game where you can make nearly everything yourself. That means your economy works best when it **adds convenience and goals**, not when it tries to replace the game.

## What makes Survival special

- **Players can farm anything.** The shop competes with the world.
- **Automation is a core part of the game.** Iron farms, crop farms and mob grinders run all day.
- **Land is a real asset.** Claims, plots and a good base location matter.
- **Villager trading** is its own economy, and players can get ahead using it.
- **Players trade items more than coins.** Many deals are item for item.

## Rule one: do not pay for what machines make

Machines make cobblestone, sugar cane, kelp, bone meal, gunpowder, iron and more. If the shop buys those, a farm turns into an endless faucet. Either:

- Do not buy them at all, or
- Buy them at a very low price with a tight daily cap.

Pay for things that need **effort and skill**: rare ores, rare drops, exploration finds, special crops and crafted goods.

## Faucets

| Faucet | Risk | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shop sales | High | Keep the list short and capped |
| Jobs | Medium to high | Pay for valuable actions, not trivial ones |
| Mob rewards | High | Mob grinders. Cap per hour, ignore spawner mobs |
| Quests and bounties | Medium | Fun but limit repeats |
| Daily and vote rewards | Medium | Small and gated by playtime |
| Player shops | None (transfer) | Money moves between players, not created |
| Villager trading | Indirect | Emeralds can be a hidden currency. See below |

## Sinks

- **Land claims and plots:** a one-time price plus ongoing upkeep.
- **Shop rent** for player shop areas.
- **Enchanting and repair costs** (admin enchant shops).
- **Rank-ups and kits.**
- **Cosmetics and titles.**
- **Teleports and warps.**
- **Seasonal items.**
- **Server projects:** community builds that need donations.

## Villagers and emeralds

Villager trading can create items and emeralds that skip your economy completely. Options:

- Accept it. Emeralds become a second currency and players trade them in the market.
- Limit villager farms and trading halls with a plugin or server rules.
- Allow emeralds in the shop at a fair price so that they connect to your coin.

The important point is to **decide**, not to be surprised.

## Land is your best sink

Land is limited and everyone needs it. Make it cost something and keep costing something:

- Initial claim cost scaled by size
- Weekly or monthly upkeep
- Bigger claims cost more per block
- Prime areas (near spawn) cost more
- Abandoned claims expire

That makes inactive players leave room and gives rich players something real to spend on.

## Admin shop for Survival

- Keep sell list small.
- Buy things like rare ores, special crops and exploration items at modest prices.
- Sell basic items cheaply so new players can get going.
- Do not sell what you want players to trade with each other.

## Player economy

Survival thrives when players trade. Help it:

- A good auction house.
- Shop districts with rent.
- A currency players trust.
- Spawn market events and shop contests.

## Seasonal wipes

Many survival servers wipe on a schedule. A fresh map is a fresh economy. If you wipe:

- Reset balances too, or the old rich dominate the new world.
- Keep cosmetics, titles and a hall of fame.
- Reuse what worked, fix what did not.

## Checklist for Survival

- Shop buys only hand-gathered or skill items
- Machine output sells at the minimum or not at all
- Land has an upkeep sink
- Villager trading has a plan
- Cap job and mob income
- Late-game sinks: cosmetics, large claims, projects
- Weekly review of the top earners
