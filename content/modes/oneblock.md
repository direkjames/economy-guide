---
title: Oneblock
desc: Economy advice for a server where everyone mines the same single block. Resources are endless, so the clicks are the real currency.
---

In Oneblock, each player stands on a tiny island and breaks one block that keeps coming back with new blocks, mobs and chests. That creates a very different economy from normal survival.

## What makes Oneblock special

- **Resources are infinite, but limited by clicks.** The real limit is how fast a player can break blocks.
- **Players start with almost nothing**, so early game money is slow and every coin matters.
- **Everything is concentrated.** Players do the same thing for hours, so one overpriced drop can dominate.
- **Space is tiny.** There is no land to buy in the usual sense, so sinks must come from upgrades.
- **Auto-clickers and AFK** are the biggest threat, because breaking the same block is easy to automate.

## Price by clicks, not by rarity

Time yourself. In a test island, how many blocks does a normal player break in an hour at each phase? That is your **blocks per hour** number. Use it to set the base value.

> [!EXAMPLE] A clicks-based price
> If a player breaks about 2,000 blocks an hour and an hour should earn 1,500 coins, then an average block is worth about 0.75 coins. A rare drop that appears once every 100 blocks should be worth a lot more than 75 coins, because it is rarer and more useful.

## Sell prices

- **Common drops** (dirt, stone, sand, cobblestone): sell for almost nothing or not at all. They arrive by the thousand.
- **Mid-value drops** (logs, ores, crops): moderate prices with daily caps.
- **Rare drops** (special blocks and chest loot): higher prices, and a cap per day.
- **Mob drops**: cap them, because mobs appear in bursts.

## Faucets to watch

| Faucet | Risk | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selling block drops | Very high | Daily caps, falling prices |
| Mob rewards | High | Cap per hour, anti-AFK |
| Phase or milestone rewards | Medium | One-time, scale with phase |
| Daily quests | Medium | Cap and scale |
| Island level rewards | Medium | One-time per level |

## Sinks that work in Oneblock

- **Island expansion** (bigger platform, extra layers, more space for farms)
- **Phase or level unlocks** that cost coins as well as clicks
- **Team slots and island members**
- **Island upgrades**: faster regeneration, extra loot chance, a storage chest
- **Cosmetics** for the island, the block and the player
- **Generators and upgrades** that cost money to build and run
- **Visitor perks and warps**
- **Prestige upgrades** at high phases

Since players spend most of their time on one island, upgrades there are a natural sink.

## Auto-clickers and AFK

Because the action is just clicking, an auto-clicker can pay for hours while a player sleeps.

- Use an anti-cheat that detects auto-clickers.
- Pay less for sustained clicking without movement or breaks.
- Use an AFK system that stops rewards when idle.
- Keep a cap per hour so that even a perfect click bot earns a bounded amount.

## Island-level economies

Many Oneblock servers use an island level system. Treat it as part of the economy:

- Each block placed adds points. Make high-value blocks worth more points, but cost coins to obtain.
- Rewards for reaching a level should be items or one-time money, not repeating payouts.
- Leaderboards drive spending on prestige blocks. That is a sink.

## Starting out

New players have nothing. Make the first hour smooth:

- A small starter kit
- A quick first phase with fast rewards
- A first goal that takes under an hour (the first expansion or a basic rank)
- A shop that buys what the first phase gives, at a low but fair price

## Checklist for Oneblock

- Measure blocks per hour at each phase
- Set sell prices from that rate
- Cap or remove sales of very common blocks
- Cap rewards for kills and clicks
- Create at least three upgrade sinks players want
- Enable anti-AFK and an auto-clicker check
- Review sales volume weekly
